### Likely Interview or Technical-Test Questions

1. **Walk me through a Blender asset you built from brief to delivery.** Outline reference selection, blockout, modeling decisions, UV/material work, lighting, review rounds, and handoff. Use a real finished asset; state your exact role and tools.
2. **How would you optimize a scene that renders too slowly?** Explain how you would reproduce and profile the issue, isolate heavy geometry/shaders/lights, test render settings and resolution, make one change at a time, and compare measured time and image quality. Avoid unmeasured improvement claims.
3. **How do you approach UVs and texturing for a reusable asset?** Describe seams, texel density, UV packing/padding, texture resolution, color space, material naming, and how you inspect distortion. Use actual examples or say which parts you are still learning.
4. **How do you document a scene for an async collaborator?** Walk through version, file and collection naming, dependencies, render settings, asset paths, known issues, export locations, and a concise handoff note. Share a sample record, not an unsupported past claim.
5. **What would you do if a Geometry Nodes setup or animation behaved inconsistently?** Reproduce with a minimal test, inspect inputs and transforms, isolate the graph/modifier, check evaluation and cache assumptions, document the fix, and validate multiple cases. Explain only tools you have personally used.
