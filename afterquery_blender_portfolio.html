01 — Portable Audio Recorder

Product hero render · Hard-surface modeling

Brief
Build a compact recorder with display, control wheel, ports, and speaker grille.

Role and tools
Self-directed model, materials, scene setup, and render. Blender 5.2.4, Cycles CPU, bevels, weighted normals, and smart UV unwrap.

Workflow
Blockout → separate body and controls → edge treatment → materials and UVs → studio lights → camera/render → save editable scene.

Technical challenge solved
Small controls needed to read at the hero camera distance. Separate meshes, controlled bevels, and contrasting emissive screen details keep the layout legible.

Measured result
40 mesh objects · 5,686 evaluated vertices · 11,214 triangles · 900 × 675 render.

Included
Beauty render and editable Blender scene. Render time is from this CPU render run, not a cross-machine benchmark.

02 — Modular Sci-Fi Utility Panel

Reusable hard-surface asset set

Brief
Create six wall modules with vents, status lights, and fasteners.

Role and tools
Self-directed kit modeling, materials, scene setup, and render. Blender 5.2.4, Cycles CPU, bevels, weighted normals, and smart UV unwrap.

03 — Stylized Desk Plant

Organic modeling and silhouette study

Brief
Create a compact decorative plant with a tapered pot, branching stems, and stylized leaves.

Role and tools
Self-directed model, materials, lighting, and render. Blender 5.2.4, Cycles CPU, editable meshes and curve stems.

04 — Three-Point Lighting Study

One object, three lighting moods

Brief
Render the same portable recorder with soft studio, dramatic, and cool technical lighting.

Role and tools
Self-directed lighting, render setup, and comparison. Blender 5.2.4, Cycles CPU, fixed camera and materials.

Workflow
Lock object/camera → create three labeled light rigs → render each setup → compare exposure, reflections, and color separation.

Technical challenge solved
Make lighting differences legible while holding product and camera constant. The scene stores all three named light sets.

Measured result
40 mesh objects · 11,214 triangles. Render times: soft 26.21 s; dramatic 27.27 s; cool 27.99 s at 900 × 675 and 20 samples.

05 — Procedural Cable and Connector Kit

Curve workflow and Geometry Nodes exploration

Brief
Create two reusable cable paths with connector housings, sleeves, and end details.

Role and tools
Self-directed asset, Geometry Nodes setup, and render. Blender 5.2.4, Cycles CPU, curve-to-mesh node group.

Workflow
Model connectors → define Bezier paths → build Curve to Mesh profile node group → assign it to both cable curves → render.

Technical challenge solved
Maintain a consistent profile along curved paths. A shared Geometry Nodes group converts each curve through a 10-segment circle with 0.045-unit radius.

Measured result
21 mesh objects · 7,876 evaluated vertices · 15,698 triangles · 25.81 s render at 900 × 675 and 20 samples.

Included
Beauty render, editable .blend with node group, and scene metrics.
